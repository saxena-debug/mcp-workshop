EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Employees can claim reimbursement for business-related expenses with receipts.
Meals during business travel: up to $50 per day.
Hotel accommodation: up to $200 per night.
Flight bookings must be economy class for domestic and business class for flights over 8 hours.
All expenses above $500 require manager approval before being incurred.
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred.
Reimbursements are processed on the 15th of every month.
