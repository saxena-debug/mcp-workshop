LEAVE POLICY
Annual Leave: All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per year.
Sick Leave: Employees may take up to 10 days of paid sick leave per year with a medical certificate.
Maternity Leave: 16 weeks of paid maternity leave for all female employees.
Paternity Leave: 5 days of paid paternity leave.
Leave must be applied for at least 3 days in advance except for emergency sick leave.
Unused annual leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 10 days.
